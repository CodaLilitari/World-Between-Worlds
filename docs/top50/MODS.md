## First five: source-specific work plan

- **SBX JK 3.1:** compare against ShadowX's native MotS 3.0 first. Audit saber slot changes, all added hotkeys, force allocation, models/sounds and deliberate player/AI changes. A renamed GOB or Ninja weapon mapping would not preserve this mod. Four original Word main text bodies have now been extracted and the feature/credit/permission sections reviewed; [the SBX comparison](SBX_REVIEW.md) records the distinction from native MotS 3.0.
- **Killer's Weapons Pack:** preserve both readme authors, ID_Junkguy and ID_Matt. Its ten weapon controllers, six network helper scripts, grapple, taunt, player code, inventory and static templates need separate exact MotS translation. The readme already reports zoom/view restoration defects, so those become explicit respawn/camera tests.
- **JKrpg - Barons Hed:** treat the episode and startup GOB together. Its player classes, money, inventory drops, shop interactions, AI, elevators and custom hotkeys need an explicit persona/bin plan and two-peer synchronization tests. Do not silently convert only the first archive.
- **Ninja Kage 7.0:** keep the existing separately selectable prototype and its documented limitations. It is not the default translator for other gameplay mods.
- **GaS:** preserve its custom gun behavior and skins in one explicit mod profile. Review each renamed weapon, mine type, projectile/helper COG, camera action and pickup ownership; the author says it contains no levels.

## Initial reusable conversion work

The initial material-only conversion batch is implemented in `mods_catalog.py` and `mods_converter.py`: Black and Yellow Flame Saber (`mod-flame-saber`) and 16-bit Saber Pack (`mod-16bit-sabers`). Exact source ZIP/GOB/member hashes and corresponding native MotS target paths are checked; internal resource paths and payload bytes are preserved; each produces a separately selected `.goo` startup package. Both complete output trees were rebuilt twice with identical bytes. These recipes make no weapon, inventory or Force substitutions. Their seven synthetic adapter checks pass; engine appearance and multiplayer testing remain separate.

Subsequent families need their own declared mechanisms: additive skin lists with native MotS model/persona preservation; weapon/inventory remapping with exact per-script edits; movement and custom hotkey ports; and combined mod-plus-level recipes with all episodes preserved. Executable installers remain passive research inputs and must never be run by WBW. No family-wide acceptance may bypass source hashes, internal path checks or authored behavior tests.

Structural/resource verification, deterministic replay, rendering, authored gameplay and multiplayer evidence remain distinct release gates under [the resource workflow](../RESOURCE_WORKFLOW.md). No download count or acquisition result establishes compatibility.

## First material recipes: scoped runtime evidence

The two optional material recipes (`mod-flame-saber` and `mod-16bit-sabers`) passed separate 30-second hidden MotS multiplayer host-load runs on native `JKM_MP/k1`, each with only its own startup overlay. The engine reported MotS multiplayer mode, advancing simulation, the expected mounts, unchanged inputs and no resource diagnostics. Their original source payloads rebuild identically across two clean runs; seven synthetic converter tests passed.

[Exact source-independent hashes and gate results](mods-host-load-evidence.json) identify both the game payload and the revision-stamped archive actually tested. These checks do **not** establish selected saber appearance, correct rendering, remote-player appearance or peer synchronization. Those remain open prototype checks.

## Larger gameplay family: KWP review

Killer's Weapons Pack is under [a separate semantic review](KWP_REVIEW.md). Its exact original ZIP now produces a repeatable 41-operation weapon-controller review across all ten weapons. It remains **unselectable and incomplete**: native player initialization, inventory/helper bins, templates, UI, dependency closure and clean runtime validation still need assembly and review. No executable game archive is produced by the maintainer review tool.

M.A.C. is also recoverable without executing its historical installer: passive extraction recovered its GOB and both manuals. Their declared sizes and CRCs match, and the GOB parser accepts all 350 entries. The wrapper still carries a header error/trailing-data warning; the inventory records that limitation and the exact extractor/source/member hashes.
